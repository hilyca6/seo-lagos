What is Lagos Life?

01 — THE GAME
A browser game set in a city that feels familiar, even if you have never been there.

Lagos Life is a free-to-start life-simulation game set in a virtual version of Lagos, Nigeria. You open it in a web browser, create an account, and move through a shared world whose landmarks and systems reference everyday life in the city.

The game’s public pages credit Shalom Rayhamen as creator. Players often search for a “Lagos lifestyle game” or a “Lagos Life sims game” — those phrases point to this same browser title, whose setting is a big part of its appeal.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lagos Life?

Lagos Life is a free life-simulation game played in your browser. It is set in Lagos, Nigeria, and is often compared with The Sims.

Is Lagos Life free to play?

The game can be opened and started for free in a browser. The official site may offer optional in-game purchases; check its current terms before paying for anything.

Do I need to download Lagos Life?

No download is needed. It runs on its official website in a browser.

Is Lagos Life a Roblox game?

No. Lagos Life is a standalone browser game on its own website. Roblox is not required.

What can you do in Lagos Life?

You build a Sim, take a job, pay rent, travel the city, and take part in shared systems such as the governor election.
