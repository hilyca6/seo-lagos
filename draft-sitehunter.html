Frequently asked

Is Lagos Life free?

Yes. Signing up and playing are free in a browser; the official site may offer optional top-ups.

Is there a download or APK?

No. It is a browser game with no official app. Add the website to your home screen for an app-like icon.

When is rent due?

Rent is charged every Saturday, so keep the money aside before you spend.
