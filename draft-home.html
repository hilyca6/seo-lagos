The game

What is Lagos Life?

A free browser life-simulation game set in a shared, Lagos-inspired city. You create a Sim, take a job, pay rent every Saturday, and move through a world with real players. People often search for it as the “Lagos Life Sims game”.

Why it spread

Three reasons

  • Instant access — one link, no download.
  • Local humour — danfo fares, Saturday rent, NEPA.
  • Shared city — visit friends, chat, vote in the election.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Lagos Life free to play?

Yes. It runs free in a browser; the official site may offer optional top-ups.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It is browser-only. Add the site to your home screen for an app-like icon.

When is rent due?

Rent is charged every Saturday — keep the money aside before you spend.

Is it a Roblox game?

No. It is a standalone browser game with its own account.
